HEFESTO — LOGO BRIEF, DESIGN GUIDELINES & GENERATION PROMPT
===========================================================
Target: docs/brand/  (layout mirrors mercury/docs/brand/README.md)
Drafted: 2026-08-19


1. CONCEPT
----------
Hephaestus (Gr. Hephaistos / pt. Hefesto) — smith-god of fire, the forge, and
the fusion of metals. The evolution this brand asserts: THE NEW FIRE IS
ELECTRICITY. Where the old god shaped bronze with flame and hammer, this one
shapes machines with current.

The detail that makes the name more than decoration: Hephaestus did not only
forge objects. In Iliad 18 he forges AUTOMATA — self-moving golden attendants
"with intelligence in their hearts" — and tripods on wheels that roll to the
gods' assembly unbidden. He is the first builder of thinking machines in the
Western canon. A framework that drives AI agents through a forge pipeline
(spec -> plan -> tasks -> code) carries exactly the right patron.

Brand story, one line:
    The old fire shaped metal. The new fire shapes machines that think.

Tone: industrial, precise, slightly severe. NOT mystical, NOT a bearded-man
illustration, NOT a flame cliche. This is a workshop, not a temple.


2. CHOSEN MARK — "the struck anvil"
-----------------------------------
An anvil silhouette, squarely planted. A lightning bolt descends as the
hammer-stroke and meets its face. Sparks leave the point of impact as short
straight ticks. (Revised 2026-08-19 - concentric arcs rejected, see sec. 9.)

Why it works:
  - The anvil is where raw material becomes a shaped thing through repeated
    strikes — the pipeline's own metaphor, and it reads as a solid mass at
    32px where thin illustration would dissolve.
  - The bolt REPLACES the hammer. That substitution is the whole thesis in one
    silhouette: same craft, new fire.
  - The spark ticks are deliberately NOT the sibling mark's nested arcs
    (mercury: three feed lines in, three broadcast arcs out) - see sec. 9.

Read at a glance: something is being forged, and the thing doing the forging
is electricity.


3. ALTERNATES (if the anvil proves too heavy)
---------------------------------------------
  A. "Bolt-hammer" — a smith's hammer in profile whose head is a lightning
     bolt. Lighter, more dynamic, weaker at small sizes.
  B. "Arc gap" — two electrode tips facing each other, the negative space
     between them forming an anvil silhouette; a single arc jumps the gap.
     Cleverest option, highest risk of reading as nothing at 32px.
  C. "Circuit forge" — anvil drawn entirely as PCB traces with via-dots at the
     corners. On-the-nose for a dev tool; may date quickly.

Recommendation: build the chosen mark first, and A as the fallback.

WARNING ON THE SECOND ACCENT: cyan tells the story, ember is nostalgia. Two
accents fighting inside a 32px icon reads as noise. Use ember ONLY as one or
two small spark ticks near the impact point, and DROP IT ENTIRELY from the
mono cut. If the icon looks busy, ember is what to delete first.


6. DESIGN GUIDELINES
--------------------
DO
  - Build from SVG primitives: <path>, <line>, <circle>, <polygon>. The sibling
    icon is 828 bytes because it uses nothing else. Aim for under 2 KB.
  - Use stroke-linecap="round" on arcs and strokes; keep stroke-width in the
    24-32 range on a 512 viewBox so it survives downscaling.
  - Keep the mark inside a 512x512 viewBox with ~40px optical padding.
  - Make the bolt a filled polygon, not a stroked zigzag — strokes thin out
    and vanish at small sizes.
  - Test at 32x32 before anything else. If the bolt/anvil relationship is not
    readable there, the mark has failed regardless of how it looks at 512.
  - Keep light and dark cuts geometrically IDENTICAL; only fill/stroke values
    differ, so a palette change is one find-and-replace per file.

DON'T
  - No gradients, no filters, no drop shadows, no blur. They break at small
    sizes, bloat the file, and render inconsistently in GitHub's sanitiser.
  - No <text> elements anywhere.
  - No raster embeds, no base64, no external references of any kind.
  - No literal flames, no anthropomorphic god, no hammer-and-anvil clip art.
  - No concentric or nested arcs anywhere in the mark - see section 9.
    count.
  - Do not put a background rectangle behind the mark; transparency is required
    so it sits on both GitHub canvases.

8. THE PROMPT (copy-paste into an SVG-capable generator)
--------------------------------------------------------
Design a minimal, industrial logo mark and deliver it as hand-written, valid
SVG. No commentary, output the SVG only.

SUBJECT
An anvil in flat silhouette, planted square and heavy, seen from the side with
its horn to the left. A lightning bolt descends vertically from the top of the
frame and strikes the anvil's face, taking the place a hammer would occupy.
Sparks leave the impact point as two or three SHORT STRAIGHT TICKS at differing
angles. Do NOT draw concentric or nested curved arcs anywhere in the mark.

MEANING TO ENCODE
Hephaestus, god of the forge, re-imagined for the age of electricity: the same
craft, powered by a new fire. The bolt substituting for the hammer is the
central idea — keep that substitution unmistakable.

STYLE
Flat vector. Geometric, confident, slightly severe. Industrial, not mystical.
No illustration, no character, no decorative flourish. Think trade-mark stamped
into steel, not an emblem painted on a shield.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (strict)
- viewBox "0 0 512 512", width and height 512, roughly 40px optical padding.
- Use only <path>, <polygon>, <line>, <circle>. No <text>, no gradients, no
  filters, no shadows, no external references, no embedded raster, no
  background rectangle. Transparent background.
- The bolt must be a FILLED polygon, not a stroked zigzag.
- Arcs: stroke, stroke-linecap="round", stroke-width between 24 and 32.
- Total file under 2 KB. Legible and unambiguous when rendered at 32x32.

COLOUR — produce three separate files
- LIGHT: anvil #2b2f36, bolt and arcs #06b6d4.
- DARK:  anvil #c9d1d9, bolt and arcs #38bdf8. Geometry identical to LIGHT.
- MONO:  everything #2b2f36, single colour, no accent.

THEN produce a 1280x320 banner in light and dark cuts: the icon at the left at
roughly 200x200 with even vertical centring, the wordmark "Hefesto" to its
right in a bold industrial grotesque, and beneath the wordmark the tagline
"Spec-driven development for Claude Code" in a medium weight at the muted
colour (#667085 light, #8b949e dark). ALL LETTERFORMS MUST BE CONVERTED TO
<path> OUTLINES — no <text> elements and no font references of any kind.


9. TESTED AND REJECTED
----------------------
Recorded 2026-08-19 after rendering candidates rather than reasoning about them.

CONCENTRIC ARCS  (was recommended in sections 2, 6 and 8 of the first draft)
  Three nested arcs radiating from one origin ARE the Wi-Fi / signal-strength
  glyph. On an anvil they do not read as current; they read as a router. This
    was confirmed by rendering, not predicted.

    REMOVED 2026-08-19 from all five cuts (radii 36/68/100 above the anvil).
    Before/after renders at 200/48/32px confirm the collision is gone and the
    mark is materially cleaner at small sizes. Do not reintroduce concentric
    arcs in any variant.

TWO ACCENTS AT SMALL SIZE
  The ember dot (#f97316, r=12) becomes an indistinct speck below ~48px while
  still costing a colour. Cyan carries the concept alone. Drop ember from the
  icon cuts before the banner.
